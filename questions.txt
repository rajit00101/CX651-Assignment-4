(1) In plot-single.png, one 500x500 multiplication took 0.200499 seconds and used 99% CPU. With ten 500x500 jobs running concurrently in plot-multi.png, the jobs averaged 0.331870 seconds and 65.2% CPU. The concurrent jobs took about 66% longer on average and received less CPU because they competed for the VM's CPUs. Unlike the steady size-related increase in plot-single.png, the variation among equal-size jobs in plot-multi.png reflects scheduling and contention.

(2) Restricting all ten jobs to one CPU made them much slower and more uniform. The taskset jobs averaged 1.960046 seconds and 9.7% CPU, compared with 0.331870 seconds and 65.2% CPU in plot-multi.png. This is about 5.9 times slower because all ten processes had to time-share one CPU instead of running across the VM's available CPUs.

(3) Plot-nice.png separates the jobs by scheduling priority. Runs 1-5, with niceness 0, averaged 0.255974 seconds and 82.0% CPU, while runs 6-10, with niceness 10, averaged 0.353169 seconds and 56.4% CPU. The higher-priority jobs finished about 28% sooner and received more CPU. Compared with plot-multi.png, changing niceness shifted CPU time toward the niceness-0 group and away from the niceness-10 group.
